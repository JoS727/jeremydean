About

Roots, rock, island soul

Jeremy Dean is a reggae musician out of San Diego. His sound blends roots reggae, rock, and island soul with authentic San Diego energy.

Live shows bring the island to the coast. Acoustic sets, full band performances, and collaborations with artists across the San Diego music scene.

Music that moves you. From the beach to the stage, Jeremy Dean brings roots, rhythm, and soul to every performance.
